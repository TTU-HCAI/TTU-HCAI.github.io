Connecting Systems to Automation

KJNdustries Warehouse Integration is a consulting business focused on helping clients connect enterprise systems, warehouse management systems, and warehouse automation technologies into one clear operating model.

The goal is to help warehouses and distribution operations reduce disconnects between software platforms and automation equipment. That includes better alignment between ERP, WMS, warehouse control systems, robotics, conveyors, and material handling solutions.

Core Services

  • Warehouse systems and automation integration planning
  • Requirements definition and process alignment
  • Interface mapping between business systems and automation vendors
  • Support for testing, deployment planning, and operational readiness

Why It Matters

Warehouse automation programs often involve multiple vendors, multiple systems, and many different handoffs between operations and technology teams. A clear integration approach helps reduce confusion, improve reliability, and make implementation more consistent across sites.
