About the Business

KJNdustries Warehouse Integration is a small consulting firm that helps organizations bridge the gap between business software and warehouse automation. The focus is on practical integration work that supports both implementation and long-term operational success.

About the Founder

KJNdustries Warehouse Integration is shaped by real world experience in supply chain automation, warehouse systems, and technology support. The founder’s background includes working in warehouse systems projects and supporting the connection between business systems, operations, and automation solutions across distribution environments.

That experience influenced the idea behind this consulting business: helping clients simplify complex integration challenges and create a clearer path between enterprise software, warehouse management systems, and automation technologies. The perspective behind the business is also supported by continued academic work in Human-Centered AI and digital project development at Texas Tech University.

Who It Serves

The business is intended to serve distribution centers, warehouse operations teams, and IT organizations that need stronger alignment between enterprise systems and automation technologies. This includes environments using robotics, conveyors, warehouse control systems, and other automated material handling solutions.

Consulting Approach

The consulting approach starts with understanding the client’s current systems, process gaps, and integration pain points. From there, the focus shifts to requirements clarity, interface alignment, and support for testing and deployment. The intent is to keep solutions readable, realistic, and aligned to business operations instead of overcomplicating the project.
